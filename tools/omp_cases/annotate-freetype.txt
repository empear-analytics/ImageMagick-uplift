# Large-glyph cases for tools/omp_compare.sh: annotate-freetype-private.h's RasterizeGlyphBitmap region (RasterizeGlyphRow, once per glyph bitmap row), reached through RenderFreetype with the repository's TrueType font ("$root" is the repository's top level, set by omp_compare.sh). GetMagickNumberThreads' limit applies to the glyph bitmap (bitmap.rows rows of bitmap.width): at -pointsize 900 the wide letters' bitmaps are several hundred pixels on a side, above the one-thread limit of 262144 pixels. The paths: an opaque fill (GetFillColor and CompositePixelOver per pixel), a semi-transparent rotated fill, a transparent fill with a transparent stroke (BlendGlyphAlpha), and no antialiasing (the opacity rounded to 0 or 1).
-size 2600x1100 xc:white -font "$root"/PerlMagick/t/Generic.ttf -pointsize 900 -fill red -annotate +20+900 WMW -format %# info:
-size 2600x1100 xc:white -font "$root"/PerlMagick/t/Generic.ttf -pointsize 900 -fill '#0000ff80' -annotate 0x10+20+900 MW -format %# info:
-size 2600x1100 xc:white -font "$root"/PerlMagick/t/Generic.ttf -pointsize 900 -fill none -stroke none -annotate +20+900 WMW -format %# info:
-size 2600x1100 xc:white -font "$root"/PerlMagick/t/Generic.ttf -pointsize 900 +antialias -fill navy -annotate +20+900 WMW -format %# info:
